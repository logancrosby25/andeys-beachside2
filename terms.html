Booking & Cancellation Terms

24-Hour Cancellation Policy

Cancellations or rescheduling requests must be made at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time.

Babysitting

Babysitting is $25 per hour with a two-hour minimum.

Swim Lessons

Swim lessons may be 30 or 60 minutes, with a maximum lesson length of one hour.

Travel Time

A 30-minute travel buffer is reserved after appointments to allow Andey to travel between families. This time is not billed to the customer.

Confirmation

Bookings are subject to availability and confirmation. Payment receipt alone does not replace Andey’s final booking confirmation.

These service terms are intended to explain the booking rules used on this website. Additional safety, weather, refund, or service-specific policies may be added as the business finalizes them.
