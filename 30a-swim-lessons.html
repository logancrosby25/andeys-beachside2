Private swim lessons for families on 30A

Andey provides private swim instruction for families throughout the 30A and South Walton service area. Lessons can take place at an approved pool at a home, vacation rental, or other approved location, making it easier to fit swimming into a beach-week schedule.

Lessons are focused and personal, with attention to the swimmer’s comfort level, current ability, and goals.

Current private swim lesson options

  • 30-minute private lesson: $45
  • 60-minute private lesson: $60
  • Two-child private lesson: $100 for 60 minutes split between two swimmers
  • Swim lesson packages are planned but currently shown as coming soon

Swim lessons have a one-hour maximum. A 30-minute travel buffer is protected after appointments and is not billed to the family.

Important swim policies

Children under age 3: a parent or guardian must be in the water and actively participating for the entire lesson.

No-shows: if a client does not show up for a scheduled swim lesson, the full lesson price remains charged and the missed lesson is not eligible for a refund.

Cancellations: cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled start are eligible for a 90% refund, with 10% retained as the booking fee. Inside 24 hours, families should contact Andey directly.

Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

How much are private swim lessons?

A 30-minute private lesson is $45 and a 60-minute private lesson is $60. A two-child 60-minute split lesson is $100.

Can lessons happen at our vacation rental pool?

Lessons can take place at an approved pool at a home, vacation rental, or another approved location within Andey’s service area.

What if my swimmer is under age 3?

A parent or guardian must be in the water and actively participating for the entire lesson.

What happens if we miss the lesson?

A swim lesson no-show is charged at the full lesson price and is not eligible for a refund.

Looking for a babysitter during your trip?

Andey also provides vacation-friendly babysitting for families staying along 30A and South Walton.
