Pet care that comes to your 30A stay

Andey offers dog walks, drop-in pet visits, extended sitting, and overnight pet sitting for families staying along 30A and South Walton.

Pet care services & pricing

  • 30-minute dog walk: $25
  • 60-minute dog walk: $45
  • 30-minute drop-in visit: $25
  • 60-minute drop-in visit: $45
  • Extended pet sitting: $25/hour, 2-hour minimum
  • Overnight pet sitting: $70/night
  • Additional dog: +$5 per visit or walk

Drop-in visits can include feeding, fresh water, potty breaks, playtime, and basic updates based on the pet’s routine and the family’s instructions.

Before pet care begins

Please provide clear instructions for feeding, medications, leash or harness use, access, behavior concerns, emergency contacts, and veterinarian information when relevant.

Dogs must have secure walking equipment, and any known aggression, bite history, escape risk, or other safety concern must be disclosed before service.
