Vacation-friendly childcare

Babysitting that comes to your 30A stay

When your family is staying along 30A or South Walton, childcare should not add another complicated errand to the day. Andey provides babysitting at approved homes and vacation rentals so parents can enjoy dinner, a date night, beach time, errands, events, or a few quiet hours.

The goal is dependable, personal care that fits naturally into a family’s vacation schedule instead of forcing everyone to travel to a separate childcare location.

What families can book

  • Vacation-rental and in-home babysitting
  • Date-night childcare
  • Daytime or evening care when availability allows
  • Two- through six-hour booking options shown in the live scheduler
  • Family details, ages, allergies, medical or safety information, and service location collected during booking

Current babysitting rate

Babysitting starts at $25 per hour for one child. Each additional child adds $10 per hour. A two-hour minimum applies.

Designed around a 30A vacation schedule

Trips along 30A often include dinner reservations, weddings, beach days, events, and adult time that are easier when a sitter can meet the family where they are staying. Andey’s service is designed around personal communication, clear booking details, and travel throughout the stated service area.

Availability is limited by Andey’s schedule and travel time, so the live calendar is the best place to see current openings.

Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

How much is babysitting?

Babysitting is $25 per hour for one child, plus $10 per hour for each additional child. There is a two-hour minimum.

Can Andey come to our vacation rental?

Yes. Babysitting can be provided at approved homes and vacation rentals within Andey’s service area.

How far in advance should we book?

Availability can fill quickly during busy travel periods. The live scheduler shows the current appointment options.

What information do I provide when booking?

The booking flow collects family contact information, child details, service location, allergies or medical and safety information, and other details Andey should know.

Need swim lessons too?

Andey also offers private swim instruction for families who want focused water confidence and skill-building during their time on 30A.
